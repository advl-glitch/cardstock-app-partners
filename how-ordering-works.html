How Ordering Works

Everything you need to know about placing requests through the Prints by Angel retailer portal. Rules vary by partner type — find your section below.

📦
Wholesale Partners Wholesale
  • 6️⃣ Order in multiples of 6. Each in-stock design must be ordered in packs of 6 (6, 12, 18…). The order form will flag any quantities that don't meet this requirement.
  • 💵 $150 minimum on your first order. If you haven't placed a fulfilled wholesale order before, your first request must total at least $150 (pre-tax). This ensures the first print run is worthwhile for both parties.
  • 🧾 You're billed after fulfillment. An estimated total is shown at checkout, but your final invoice reflects actual quantities shipped. Virginia sales tax (5.3%) applies.
  • ✅ You own the inventory. Once shipped, cards are yours to sell at your own retail price. No revenue splits.
Wholesale pricing is shown in your cart and review screen once you've selected your store account.
🤝
Consignment Partners Consignment
  • 4️⃣ Minimum 4 cards per design. Each design in your restock request must have a quantity of at least 4. This keeps display quantities practical and tracking manageable.
  • 💰 No upfront payment. You don't pay for the cards — Angel stocks your location and you pay a percentage of sales. Your restock request triggers Angel to review and send more cards.
  • 📍 Virginia only. Consignment partnerships are currently available in select areas of Virginia. If you're outside those areas, please inquire about wholesale.
  • 📬 Angel reviews every request. Once submitted, your restock request goes to Angel for approval and fulfillment. You'll receive a confirmation email.
No pricing is shown for consignment partners — revenue sharing is handled separately per your individual agreement.
⏱
Made to Order Print Request
  • 🖨 Out-of-stock designs available to wholesale partners. Designs in the Made to Order section are currently out of stock but can be ordered — Angel will produce them for your request.
  • 📅 Allow approximately 3 weeks for production. Print requests require time for production and quality checks. Angel will confirm a ready date when approving your request.
  • 🔄 Subject to approval. Angel reviews each print request individually and may approve, decline, or adjust quantities based on production schedule and demand.
  • 📦 Same wholesale rules apply. MTO quantities must be in multiples of 6, and the $150 first-order minimum still applies if this is your first wholesale order.
🔄
The Order Process
  • 1️⃣ Browse and add to your request. Select quantities for each design you want, then click Add. Your cart builds up in the Your Request section at the bottom.
  • 2️⃣ Enter your store and submitter info. Search for your store name and fill in your name and email so Angel knows who sent the request.
  • 3️⃣ Review, then verify your identity. You'll be asked to enter the personal email or cell number of the owner or manager who set up your account. This protects against unauthorized orders.
  • 4️⃣ Confirmation email. Once submitted, you'll receive a confirmation. Angel reviews the request and will reach out with any questions or a ready date.
🃏
Card Lines
  • ✦ Standard. The core Prints by Angel catalog — available to all wholesale partners, orderable as Made to Order when out of stock.
  • ⭐ Limited Edition. Special runs produced in small quantities. Not available as Made to Order — once stock is gone, it's gone. Hidden by default; use the "Show Limited Edition" button to view them.
  • 🔞 Naughty Cards. Adult humor cards (18+). Hidden behind an age confirmation. Not available as Made to Order. Please ensure your store is an appropriate environment for this content.
Questions? Reach out to Angel directly at your usual contact info. If you need to set up a new account or have trouble logging in, use the "Not listed? Request a partner account" option on the stock page.
